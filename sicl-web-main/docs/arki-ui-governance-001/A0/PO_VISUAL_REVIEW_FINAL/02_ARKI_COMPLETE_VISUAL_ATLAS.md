# ARKI complete visual atlas — A0.R3

## Scope and evidence rule

This atlas covers every one of the 38 canonical route entries. It separates route-specific rendered screens from blocked states, non-user-facing entries and UNKNOWN entries. The complete raw capture corpus is in `screens/` and the PDF contact sheet includes all 110 4001 captures.

The browser tool names raw screenshots with timestamps and does not encode route identity. A0.R3 therefore preserves the full corpus and records route-association uncertainty rather than assigning a screenshot to the wrong route.

## Product areas

**Entry and project workspace.** First Contact, project workspace, Evidence and the decision-oriented surfaces establish project context, authority and Core state. The rendered entry and evidence states are useful for understanding the operating model, but Core-backed actions remain blocked when the canonical transport is unavailable.

**Predictive and parametric design.** Design Funnel, Parametric Generator, Family Studio, Super-Architect and Multiobjective are the strongest architect-facing surfaces in the runtime evidence. They expose alternatives, parameters, conceptual geometry, cost ranges or explicit objective comparisons. They remain conceptual and do not replace specialist calculations or signed deliverables.

**BIM and field coordination.** BIM Viewer and Host Connections render coordination-oriented concepts and human review gates. Field Mode renders a fail-closed dependency state when the session/Core path is not available.

**Federated LUCA surfaces.** LUCA Control Center and AR-LU Orchestrator render the separation between ARKI and LUCA, the shared contracts and human approval gates. ARKI-TV and Marketplace render governed/frozen or candidate states rather than autonomous publication.

**Help and operational surfaces.** Help renders operational guidance in the available evidence. Several operational/library routes remain in the lazy fallback and are not visually judged beyond that state.

## Three-panel law

For Design Funnel the captured layout follows the requested law: **left = cost/finance, center = architecture, right = selected proposal intelligence**. The center contains the architectural cards and view controls. No repair or redesign was performed.

## Visual disposition

Rendered architecture-facing screens are proposed to remain **KEEP** for the next wave. Blocked screens are **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** for visual judgment. Fail-closed Core states should remain visible safety states until the authorized transport is configured.
