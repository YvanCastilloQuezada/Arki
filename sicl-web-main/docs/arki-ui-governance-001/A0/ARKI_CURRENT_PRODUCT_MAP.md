# ARKI current product map — A0.R3

## What exists

The source declares 36 concrete routes plus two explicit catch-all entries retained by the A0 canonical map. The principal product areas are First Contact, project workspace, predictive Design Funnel, Parametric Generator, Family Studio, BIM Viewer, Super-Architect, multiobjective comparison, AR-LU/LUCA federation, field mode, help and security-oriented modules.

## What renders

The runtime evidence includes 15 route-specific rendered screens. The strongest architect-facing surfaces are Design Funnel, Parametric Generator, Family Studio, BIM Viewer, Super-Architect and Multiobjective. These screens show architecture, parameters, alternatives, costs or coordination concepts.

## What is blocked

Multiple routes remain in `Cargando módulo`, which is classified as `BLOCKED_LAZY`. Evidence also contains Core-dependent fail-closed/503 states. Authentication-dependent routes are classified separately when the browser redirects to Manus login. One declared route rendered 404 and is classified `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; routes without route-specific evidence remain `UNKNOWN`.

## What users can do

Users can inspect conceptual architecture, adjust parametric inputs, compare alternatives, inspect BIM/IFC-oriented coordination surfaces, review AR-LU governance and browse help where the route renders. They cannot be assumed to complete a Core-backed mutation, approve a decision, publish content or exercise every visible control from this audit.

## What remains unknown

The route-to-raw-screenshot association is not encoded by the browser filenames. The exact HTTP status and module evaluation sequence for lazy chunks were not persisted in the available artifacts. Visible controls have not been broadly exercised. These are UNKNOWN, not failures.

## Evidence separation

**CODE_EVIDENCE** comes from App.tsx, the A0 registry and package scripts. **RUNTIME_EVIDENCE** comes from production 4001 page text and screenshots. **UNKNOWN** is explicitly labeled where evidence is incomplete. **PROPOSAL** appears only in the disposition and repair-program documents.
